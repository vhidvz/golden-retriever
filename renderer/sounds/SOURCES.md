# Sound sources

Every recording here is in the public domain (CC0), from OpenGameArt.

| Files | Source | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bark_1.ogg` … `bark_4.ogg` (four barks cut from one recording) | https://opengameart.org/content/dog-barking-mono | Brandon Morris | CC0 |
| `sigh_1.ogg`, `dream_1.ogg` (the same short grunt) | https://opengameart.org/content/dog-grunt | qubodup | CC0 |

Buddy also knows how to use `whine`, `yawn`, `sniff`, `shake`, `eat` and `drink`
sounds, but no freely licensed recordings of those have been added yet, so he
makes them silently. To add one, drop a file named `<kind>_<anything>.ogg` or
`.wav` here or in `~/.local/share/buddy/sounds/`.
